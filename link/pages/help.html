Getting Started

Welcome to our service platform! Here's how to get started:

  • Create an account or log in to your existing account
  • Browse service categories to find what you need
  • Create a company profile to offer your services
  • View and manage your companies from your profile

Creating a Company Profile

To create a company profile:

  • Click on "Post Service" in the bottom navigation
  • Fill in all required fields (marked with *)
  • Upload your company logo (optional)
  • Provide contact information
  • Select your service category
  • Read and accept the consent terms
  • Click "Submit" to create your profile

Managing Your Companies

You can view and manage all your companies:

  • Go to "Profile" in the bottom navigation
  • View all your post in a list
  • Click on any company to view full details
  • Filter companies by category

Searching for Services

To find services:

  • Use the search bar on the home page
  • Browse categories by clicking on category cards
  • View company details by clicking on a company
  • Contact companies using the Contact Options

Account & Security

For account-related questions:

  • Make sure you're logged in to access all features
  • You can only view and edit your own companies
  • If you forget your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the login page
  • Log out when using shared devices

Common Issues

Can't create a company?

  • Make sure all required fields are filled
  • Check that you're logged in
  • Verify your internet connection

Can't see my companies?

  • Make sure you're logged in
  • Check if you have created any companies
  • Try refreshing the page
